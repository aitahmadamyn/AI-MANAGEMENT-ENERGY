# Phase 1: System Boundaries, Data Architecture & Engineering Assumptions

## 1. Control Volume & System Boundaries

The **EcoFarm Circular** pilot plant couples agricultural biomass valorization with hybrid solar and thermal energy storage. Phase 1 establishes four non-overlapping engineering boundaries before any data is downloaded or any Machine Learning model is trained.

### Boundary A — External Meteorological & Solar Environment
- **Inputs crossing boundary:** Global solar irradiance ($\text{W/m}^2$), ambient dry-bulb temperature ($^\circ\text{C}$), wind speed at $10\text{ m}$ ($\text{m/s}$), relative humidity ($\%$).
- **Data Provenance:** `MEASURED_EXTERNAL` (reanalysis / satellite-derived historical datasets from European Commission **PVGIS** and open meteorological archives).
- **Timestamp Standard:** Strictly **UTC ISO-8601** (`YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS+00:00`) at 60-minute intervals to eliminate Daylight Saving Time duplicates and timezone drift.

### Boundary B — Energy Conversion & Sand Thermal Storage
- **Photovoltaic Array:** Converts irradiance and cell temperature conditions into DC/AC electrical power ($\text{kW}$). Resistance heaters inside the sand battery may only draw **genuine surplus** PV electricity when solar generation exceeds baseline electrical loads.
- **Solar Thermal Collectors & Pyrolysis NCG Combustion:** Supply thermal power ($\text{kW}_{\text{th}}$) to the Heat-Transfer-Fluid (HTF) circuit or directly charge the sand bed.
- **Sand Thermal Battery:** Sensible heat storage governed by:
  $$Q_{\text{usable}} = \frac{m_{\text{sand}} \cdot C_p \cdot (T_{\text{sand}} - T_{\min})}{3600} \quad [\text{kWh}]$$
  with standby thermal losses $Q_{\text{loss}} = UA \cdot (T_{\text{sand}} - T_{\text{amb}})$ and strict operating bounds ($120^\circ\text{C} \le T_{\text{sand}} \le 550^\circ\text{C}$).

### Boundary C — Indirect Biomass Dryer
- **Thermodynamic Load:** Heat is transferred from the HTF circuit via an indirect heat exchanger into drying air.
- **Physical Energy Terms (developed fully in Phase 3 before synthetic data generation):**
  1. **Water Mass Removed:** $m_w = m_{\text{wet}} \cdot \frac{X_i - X_f}{1 - X_f}$
  2. **Latent Heat of Vaporization:** $Q_{\text{latent}} = m_w \cdot h_{fg}$
  3. **Sensible Heating (dry biomass + water):** $Q_{\text{sensible}} = (m_{\text{dry}} C_{p,\text{bio}} + m_{\text{wet},w} C_{p,w}) \cdot \Delta T$
  4. **Thermal Efficiency & Losses:** $Q_{\text{demand}} = \frac{Q_{\text{latent}} + Q_{\text{sensible}}}{\eta_{\text{dryer}}}$

### Boundary D — AI Advisory vs. PLC Hardwired Control
- The AI system operates exclusively in an **advisory / supervisory decision-support layer** (`MODE 1 CHARGE` through `MODE 5 AUXILIARY HEATING`).
- **Hardwired PLC Interlocks** (over-temperature trips $> 600^\circ\text{C}$, $\text{O}_2$ monitoring in pyrolysis/NCG combustion, HTF pressure & minimum flow switches, dryer spark/over-temp protection) are physically isolated from AI outputs and can **never** be bypassed by software.

## 2. Data Provenance Discipline

To prevent scientific misrepresentation and Machine Learning data leakage:

| Provenance Tag | Meaning | Examples in EcoFarm Circular |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `measured_external` | Real historical observations or satellite-derived reanalysis | Irradiance, ambient temperature, wind speed, relative humidity |
| `simulated` | Physics-based forward simulation using documented equations | Sand bed temperature, HTF loop temperatures, NCG burner schedule |
| `calculated` | Deterministic formula computed directly from inputs | Usable stored energy $Q$, dryer thermodynamic demand |
| `predicted_ml` | Forecast generated by a trained ML model (Phase 5+) | Predicted PV power ($\hat{P}_{\text{PV}}$), predicted dryer demand ($\hat{Q}_{\text{dryer}}$) |

---

## 3. Data Quality & Chronological Split Rules (For Phases 2–5)

1. **Raw vs. Processed Separation:** Raw downloads stay immutable in `data/raw/`. Cleaned, gap-checked datasets are written to `data/processed/`. Thermal simulations live separately in `data/simulated/`.
2. **Physical Range Validation:** Every column is checked against physical bounds (e.g., irradiance $\in [0, 1361]\ \text{W/m}^2$, humidity $\in [0, 100]\%$, $T_{\text{sand}} \in [20, 600]^\circ\text{C}$).
3. **Chronological Splitting (No Random Shuffling):** Time-series datasets will be split **strictly by date** (e.g., Months 1–8 Train, Months 9–10 Validation, Months 11–12 Test) to prevent future-to-past data leakage.
